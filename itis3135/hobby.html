Photography is the art of capturing light with a camera to create images. It can be a creative outlet and a way to document life.

About

I started photography five years ago, and it has become my passion. Capturing moments and telling stories through images is what I love.

Best Times

The golden hour, just after sunrise and before sunset, provides the best natural lighting for photography.

Best Locations

Nature, urban landscapes, and cultural sites are excellent places for photography.

How to Start

Begin with a smartphone camera, learn the basics of lighting and composition, and gradually invest in better equipment.

Why Photography?

Photography allows for creative expression, preserves memories, and can even become a career.
