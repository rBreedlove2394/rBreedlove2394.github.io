About Vanguard Security Solutions

Founded in the heart of Belmont, NC, Vanguard Security Solutions has provided top-notch security services for over a decade. We are committed to safety, professionalism, and community trust.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver reliable, customized security services that protect what matters most to our clients — their homes, businesses, and loved ones.

Our History

What began as a small local operation has grown into a trusted regional provider of residential and commercial security services. From basic alarm systems to advanced access control and monitoring, we’ve stayed at the forefront of security innovation.

Our Team

Our strength lies in our people. Each team member is trained, licensed, and committed to upholding the highest standards of integrity and vigilance. From field agents to support staff, we operate with professionalism and purpose.
